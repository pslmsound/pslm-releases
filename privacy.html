Privacy Policy · Last updated: 2026-09-29

PSLM is a local-first audio mastering app. Your music never leaves your device, and the app collects no personal data. The only personal information we ever handle is an email address, and only if you choose to give us one on our website — see Mailing list below.

The app

  • All audio processing (analysis, mastering, and export) happens entirely on your device. Your audio files never leave your device and are never uploaded.
  • PSLM does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • Mastering works fully offline. The app makes no network connection to do its job.
  • Update checks: so it can tell you when a new version exists, PSLM checks a small update file (our “appcast”) about once a day. That file is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web request, the host receives your IP address in order to serve it. The request sends no audio, no account, no identifiers and no system profile, and nothing about it reaches us. Updates are offered, never installed behind your back. You can turn automatic checks off in the app.
  • Links: when you click something like this policy or our website, the app hands the address to your browser. Nothing is sent in the background.
  • Microphone: PSLM requests microphone access only so the operating system can initialize the audio session used for master playback and A/B monitoring. PSLM does not record, store, or transmit any audio from your microphone.

Mailing list

If you enter your email address on our website to be told when version 1.0 is released, we store that address for that purpose. It is handled by Buttondown, the email service we use to send the message, and it is kept only as long as you stay subscribed.

  • Giving us your address is entirely optional. You never need an email address to download, run, or use PSLM.
  • We use it to email you about PSLM — nothing else.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share it.
  • You can unsubscribe from any email we send, or write to the address below and we will remove you.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we never have.
